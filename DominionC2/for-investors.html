Problem

Tactical Android and iOS devices (ATAK, COMSEC, battlefield apps) are increasingly targeted by advanced adversaries, yet there is no complete, resilient, dual‑platform C2 threat emulation tool for authorised red team operations and military cyber ranges.

Solution – DominionC2

DominionC2 is a dual‑use, multi‑agent Command & Control (C2) framework that emulates real‑world threats against Android endpoints. With this investment, we will build an iOS agent and unify both platforms into a single C2 dashboard. The platform already provides GPS, contacts, SMS, call logs, clipboard, screen/audio/video recording, shell execution, and one‑click reporting – even under degraded networks.

Key Differentiators

  • ✅ Resilient file transfer – Three‑step stabilisation + exponential backoff (RFC 7230).
  • ✅ Multi‑agent by design – Dynamic device list, auto‑refresh, sessionStorage.
  • ✅ Legal & transparent – Physical install, user prompts – not spyware.
  • ✅ Modular architecture – Easy to extend (iOS, new commands).
  • ✅ Instant reporting – CSV, PDF, XLSX exports.

Target Markets & Dual‑Use

  • Military / NATO: Cyber range training, COMSEC auditing, ATAK resilience, adversary emulation.
  • Commercial: Insider threat detection, mobile DLP validation, BYOD pentesting (banking, critical infra).
  • Red Teams & Pentesters: Authorised Android & iOS assessments.

Current Status (TRL 4+)

Fully functional Android prototype (7,145 LOC) demonstrated on AVDs and physical devices. Core features validated in lab. Next: iOS agent development and cyber range validation (TRL 5‑6).
